In my opinion, sculpture is one of my favorite ways to create art. The physical nature of an object from my imagination is the closest I can get to turning my dreams into a reality. Many of the works here were school projects with a specific direction and a goal. However, you can be certain that I shaped those assignments into something I liked and finished them into something I was proud of.



PROP - FAKE FOOD ASSIGNMENT


One of the first assignments we had for this theater prop making class I took was to make some realistic fake foods. Because of the time contraints with my other classes, I was only able to make 2 loaves of bread, but I made sure that they looked as delicious as a real loaf of bread.








PROP - STAGE COMBAT FISH


The next assignment for the same theater props making class was to create a "Stage Combat Fish", making it as close to our chosen fish as possible, while still being durable and soft enough to hit someone with it.

SCULPTURE - YELLOW JACKET HIVE


In a 3D Design Art Class I took, we began with a wireframe sculpture required to be at least 2 feet in length(or 16inches). I've always liked the appearence of a yellow jacket ever since I hunted them in my backyard.








SCULPTURE - RIBROD


For our final in the same 3D Design Art Class, our professor thought it would be interesting to task out next assignment with motion. The project required that we made an artpiece that could roll so that we could race these pieces against each other like a soapbox derby. So I found it quite fitting to create a design inspired by my fascination with Hot Wheels. And yes, it rolls, and it rolls fast.
